# T.N.W.R. Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 25, 2026*

T.N.W.R. ("The Naggy Wife Reminder") is a reminder app with an optional Alexa
skill ("Naggy Wife"). This policy explains what data it uses.

## On your device

Your reminders and settings are stored **only on your device**. The app has
no ads, analytics, or tracking. On phones, T.N.W.R. checks whether a call is in
progress so it can pause alarms. It does **not** read who you're calling or any
call details.

## If you connect Alexa (optional)

To play reminders on your Echo, the app sends the following to our server
(hosted on Supabase):

- An anonymous account ID created by the app (no name, email or password).
- Your Amazon Alexa user ID for the Naggy Wife skill, once you link it.
- The **title and due time** of reminders due in the next 7 days.

The server passes this to the Naggy Wife skill, which creates Alexa reminders on
your account and keeps a list of them so it can remove them when the task is
done. Reminder titles and times are not used for anything else, not sold, and
not shared except with Amazon to deliver the reminders.

To remove this data, tap **Settings → Alexa → Disconnect** in the app. That
removes your Echo reminders and the link. Disabling the skill in the Alexa app
also stops it.

## If you use "Photo approved by someone" (optional)

The photo you take is uploaded to a private storage area on our server,
along with the task title and the approver's first name (their phone number stays
on your phone). Only someone with the link you send can see it, through a
short-lived address. The photo is **deleted as soon as the approver taps Approve
or Not done**. The decision and any note they write are kept so the app can
show them to you.
